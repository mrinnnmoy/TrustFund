# TrustFund Brand Guidelines

## Concept

TrustFund is a transparent fundraising product with a friendly, modern editorial identity. The visual system should feel approachable enough for everyday giving while keeping on-chain information clear and trustworthy.

The core mark is a lime radial burst.

A compact symbol for collective action, momentum and many contributors meeting around one cause.

---

## Color

| Token                 | Value     | Use                            |
| --------------------- | --------- | ------------------------------ |
| `--color-paper`       | `#FFFFFF` | Main page background           |
| `--color-surface`     | `#F8F8F6` | Cards and quiet sections       |
| `--color-ink`         | `#111111` | Primary text and dark surfaces |
| `--color-muted`       | `#737373` | Secondary copy                 |
| `--color-accent`      | `#B9F34A` | Primary CTA, progress and logo |
| `--color-accent-soft` | `#D7FF7A` | Accent gradient highlight      |
| `--color-accent-dark` | `#91C92F` | Accent hover/pressed state     |
| `--color-border`      | `#E6E6E2` | Hairline borders and dividers  |
| `--color-error`       | `#B42318` | Errors only                    |

The primary accent treatment is `linear-gradient(90deg, #B9F34A 0%, #D7FF7A 100%)`.

**Do:** Keep the interface predominantly white/black and let lime provide the energy.

**Don't:** Introduce competing bright CTA colors.

---

## Typography

- **Manrope :** Display, headings, body copy and UI labels.
- **IBM Plex Mono :** Literal on-chain values only like SOL amounts, wallet addresses, transaction identifiers and timestamps derived from chain state.

Use strong scale contrast like large editorial headings, compact supporting copy and generous whitespace.

---

## Shape

The UI uses soft rounded geometry rather than decorative Web3 styling.

- Hero media and major containers : Large radius.
- Cards : Medium radius.
- CTA buttons : Pill-shaped.
- Campaign images : Rounded, with clean edge alignment.

Keep effects restrained. Avoid glassmorphism, neon glows and excessive gradients.

---

## Logo usage

- `logo-full.svg` : Primary TrustFund lockup for navigation, footer and marketing surfaces.
- `logo-icon.svg` : Standalone burst for compact placements and avatars.
- `favicon.svg` : Simplified browser/favicon asset.

Keep clear space around the burst equal to roughly one inner-circle diameter. Do not distort, rotate, outline or add shadows to the logo. Use the supplied artwork rather than recoloring individual rays.

---

## Photography

Use human, documentary-style fundraising imagery : Hands, communities, volunteers, beneficiaries and real-world action. Images should feel optimistic and immediate rather than corporate or crypto-centric.

---

## Voice

TrustFund copy is concise, active and human. Prefer direct labels such as **Donate**, **Create Campaign** and **Withdraw Funds**. Avoid crypto jargon when ordinary fundraising language communicates the action clearly.

---

## Product-data rule

The visual layer may be warm and approachable, but blockchain values must remain precise. Display literal on-chain data with the mono type treatment and never imply that indexed database data is more authoritative than Solana state.
